      How to play

      How to win

      First player to the point goal wins. 2–3 players: 10 points. 4–5 players: 6 points. 6 players: 5 points.

      Setup

      Everyone is dealt 5 cards from the 90-card playing deck. The 30 point cards sit off to the side as the point pile. Play goes clockwise.

      During your turn

      Play as many action cards as you like — there is no limit, and you may also play none. Follow the instruction on each card as you play it.

      Ending your turn

      There are exactly two ways a turn ends:

      • You earn a point. Your turn ends immediately. You do not draw and you do not play anything else.
      • You draw a card. If you are done playing and earned nothing, draw one card to end your turn. You cannot play it until next turn.

      FREE POINT, STEAL A POINT and DRAW 3 also end your turn once they resolve.

      Character cards

      Lay down a matching set of 3 to score:

      • 3 CHARLIES = 1 point
      • 3 SPIKES = 2 points
      • 3 WALTERS = 3 points
      • 3 WILDS = 3 points

      A WILD stands in for any character card. A set can never be STOPPED.

      Action cards

      STOP

      Any player may play STOP at any time to cancel any action card. A STOP can be cancelled by another STOP, and so on — an odd number of STOPs kills the action, an even number lets it through. STOP cannot cancel a set of character cards.

      If FREE POINT or STEAL A POINT is stopped, no point changes hands and it is still that player's turn.

      Playing with friends

      Open a room and send anyone the link. As they arrive they take a seat, and the host can slide the seat count up or down at any point before the deal — every seat nobody claims is played by a bot.

      Turn up after the cards are out and you can take over a bot instead: you inherit its hand, its points and its place in the order. If someone puts their phone down mid-game, a bot covers their seat after 30 seconds so the table never stalls, and they get it straight back when they return.

      Other rules

      • Who starts — the first turn is tossed for, not given to whoever dealt.
      • Running out of cards — the moment your hand is empty your turn is over. You pick up 3 at the start of your next turn, and that turn ends there too.
      • If the draw pile runs out, the discard pile is shuffled to make a new one.
      • Points are always visible to everyone — tap 🏆 for the full standings.
